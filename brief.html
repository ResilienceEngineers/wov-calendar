How to read this page — open if you're new here

The deep view complements the executive one-pager with full data layers and per-cluster detail.

  • Severity heatmap — 31-day forward look, one row per risk family (weather hazards + cargo crime + cyber + conflict + labour). Darker = higher severity that day. Today is the red-outlined column.
  • Map — toggle layers. Polygons = pattern bounding boxes. Red dots = active events. Amber dots = force-majeure. White dots = monitored chokepoints. Persian-Gulf cluster = upstream Hormuz snapshot.
  • Convergence clusters — tabbed by axis, filterable by region. The chokepoint tab includes single-event observations (Theory of Constraints). Other axes need 2+ overlapping bands.
  • Custom analyses — your commodity-impact chains with cascade + timeline.

Scoring & windows. The window shown is the peak-overlap period — when the most bands peak simultaneously — not the full season. Score 0–10 is additive across five bounded parts: depth (how many peak together), severity, concentration (shared industries × commodities), signal reliability, and chokepoint criticality. Surface threshold 5.0. The ENSO modulator is context only and does not drive clusters.

Methodology

This calendar uses a tiered signal-weighting framework. Only verified physical events from primary sources shape band creation. Recurring patterns are evidence-based, catalogued in a single-source-of-truth CSV, and tracked fortnightly for materialisation. Convergence scoring runs deterministically over the union of pattern, active-event, force-majeure, and analysis bands. The full operating ruleset is kept private to preserve analytical edge.
