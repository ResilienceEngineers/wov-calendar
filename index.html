Methodology & sources

This calendar uses a tiered signal-weighting framework. Only verified physical events from primary sources can shape band creation. Convergence scoring runs deterministically over the union of seasonal, active, FM, and user-analysis bands. The full operating ruleset is kept private to preserve analytical edge. Six tiers of source attribution: T1 primary/official; T2 specialised commercial intelligence; T3 analytical institutions; T4 commercial analysts with track records; T5 wire services and regional press; T6 excluded by default.
