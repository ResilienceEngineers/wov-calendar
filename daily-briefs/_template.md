# Weekly WoV delta — YYYY-MM-DD (week N)

Narrative companion to the data layer. Written on Fridays by the weekly calibration run. Captures what *changed* this week — not a snapshot of the calendar (the JSON files are that).

## Headline

- New clusters that crossed the surface threshold (>=5.5) this week
- Clusters that dropped below threshold (de-escalation or expiry)
- Single most material new band registered

## Convergences scored

- Brier score on cluster `confidence` predictions
- Hit / miss / false-alarm tally for clusters surfaced 7, 14, 30 days ago
- Surprises (realized impacts with no cluster at T-7)

## Source adjustments

Sources whose reliability score moved this week, with the trigger event.

## Methodology delta

One concrete change to scoring, triage rules, or vocabulary. Logged in `methodology.md` §11.

## Watching next week

- Clusters trending toward surface threshold
- Seasonal bands entering peak window in the next 30 days
- Vocabulary gaps flagged by triage that need adjudication
